# Storefront Builder — Quick-start Guide

How to design and publish your online store in the Silarai dashboard. Shoppers see nothing you change until you press **Publish**.

## Before you start
- **Products and categories** are added (*Catalog*). Templates and AI use your real products.
- **Storefront settings** (sidebar *Storefront*): check your **Store URL** and upload your **Store Logo**. Your store lives at `app.silarai.com/<your-store-url>` (or your own domain under *Custom Domain*).

## 1. Open the builder
Sidebar → **Customise store**. It opens full screen:
- **Left:** Sections · Style · Pages · Menus
- **Middle:** live preview (desktop / tablet / phone buttons in the top bar)
- **Right:** settings for whatever you selected

The first time, the **template wizard** opens automatically.

## 2. Pick a template
1. Answer *What do you sell?*
2. Pick a template. The preview shows it with your own logo and products.
3. Pick colours and fonts.

| Template | Best for |
|---|---|
| Starter | Simple default for any store |
| Noor | Jewellery, luxury |
| Mandi | Grocery, daily needs |
| Bloom | Fashion, beauty, gifts |
| Crumb | Bakery, food |
| Volt | Electronics, gadgets |

To switch later: *Style → Template → Change*. You can **keep your pages** or **use the template's pages**.

## 3. Edit sections
- Click any section in the preview, or in the *Sections* list, to edit it on the right: **layout**, **colour scheme**, text, images, products and more.
- **Add section:** pick from the gallery. Your saved sections appear first, then your template's exclusive widgets.
- Use the section toolbar to **move, duplicate, hide or remove** a section. *Show on* lets you hide a section on phone or desktop.
- **Save for reuse:** saves a section so you can insert it again later (up to 50 per store).

## 4. Style your store
*Style* tab:
- **Style kit:** the overall look.
- **Palette:** colours. You can also make a custom palette; it checks that text stays readable.
- **Fonts**, plus fine-tune options.
- **Store behaviour:**
  - How products open (popup, full page, or both)
  - Card style
  - Cart style (drawer, page, popup, bottom sheet) and its options: free-delivery bar, upsell, coupon field
  - Compare
  - Floating buttons

## 5. Pages and menus
- *Pages* tab:
  - **Home**
  - **Catalogue & categories**
  - **Product page**
  - **Other product designs:** a different product page for chosen categories or products
  - **Custom pages:** About, FAQ, Contact, policies, and so on, each with its own title, URL and SEO settings
- *Menus* tab: edit the main menu (3 levels) and the footer menu (2 levels).

## 6. Let AI help (optional)
- *Pages → New page with AI*, or *Sections → Add sections with AI*.
- Describe what you want → **Generate** → check the preview → apply it, or **Try again**.
- AI doesn't add images and may leave `[placeholders]`. Fill those in before publishing.

## 7. Save, preview and publish
- **Autosave:** your edits save as a draft automatically. The top bar shows the save status.
- **Undo / redo:** use the top bar buttons, or Ctrl/⌘+Z and Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z.
- **Share preview:** gives a private link to your saved draft that you can send to others for review. You can turn it off at any time.
- **Publish:** puts everything live in one step. It also updates your brand colours from the palette.
- **View store:** opens the live store.
- **Version history** (clock icon): your last 10 publishes. Open one to preview it, then **Restore** to copy it back into your draft. After restoring, publish again.

## Good to know
- Unpublished changes are never visible to shoppers, except through your preview link.
- A store with nothing published shows the **Starter** template.
- The preview link shows only saved drafts, has no expiry, and you get one link per store.
- Pages made in the old page editor (before the builder) have to be rebuilt in the builder.
